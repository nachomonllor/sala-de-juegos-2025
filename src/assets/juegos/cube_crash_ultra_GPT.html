How to Play

  • 1 Click on groups of 2 or more connected cubes of the same color to break them.
  • 2 Larger groups give quadratically more points! (Score = Count² × 10).
  • 3 Cubes fall down due to gravity, and empty columns slide left to fill gaps.
  • 4 Use Power-Ups like Bomb to blast 3x3 areas, Shuffle to mix cubes, or Undo if you made a mistake.
